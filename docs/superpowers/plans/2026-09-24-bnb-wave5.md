# BNB Hack wave 5 — the ideas list, made simple

**Goal:** Cover the brief's "Ideas to Build" on BSC so a person who has never used crypto can use
every one of them: spread watch, rules Vera runs for you, a stocks + crypto mix, an earnings agent,
more baskets, a bStock-vs-Ondo board, idle cash that earns, Wallet API balances, and a Binance
dry-run before every trade.

**Base:** branch `bnb-bsc` after `baa92c1` (BNB Chain is the default). Global Constraints and Review
Focus of `2026-09-24-bnb-bsc.md` still apply.

**UX bar (user direction, 2026-09-24):** "so simple even a web2-naive person can use it." Every
screen answers, in plain words: can I buy this now, what does it cost me vs the real share, who am
I buying from, and what happens next. No "venue", "gap", "slippage", "gas", "RFQ", "bps" in copy.
Times are the viewer's local time, labelled "your time". Numbers read as sentences ("You pay 0.4%
more than the real share"), not signed percentages next to price changes.
